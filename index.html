The Immortal Collection

Limited editions of 250 numbered bottles per fragrance. One run, then retired. Each scent is not a portrait of a person but a snapshot of their time and place, the air they moved through, distilled.

Churchill

Leaders 001 · Eau de Parfum

Never surrender the room.

Dry wit, cigars, cognac and sheer resolve. Bright citrus and cracked black pepper over old library air, settling into oak shelves and a first hint of leather, closing on cigar leaf, smoky cypriol, cedar and amber.

Topbright citrus, black pepper, clove
Heartlibrary shelves, oak barrel, leather
Basecedar, amber, cigar leaf, rum, cognac, tonka

Jesse James

Rogues 001 · Extrait de Parfum

Ride fast, leave no trace.

A dangerous sweetness kept carefully low. A flash of orange peel, then saddle leather, cured tobacco and oak barrel, closing on dry cedar, trail dust, distant campfire smoke and a quiet animalic warmth.

Toporange peel
Heartsaddle leather, cured tobacco, oak barrel, tobacco-opium
Basedry cedar, trail dust, campfire smoke, animalic musk

Hypatia

Luminaries 001 · Eau de Parfum

Clarity, even in the dark.

Luminous composure under pressure. A metallic rose flash and bergamot air, then coriander, osmanthus and apricot over cool marble and parchment, closing on pale olive wood, cedar, myrrh and smoky styrax.

Topmetallic rose, bergamot
Heartcoriander, osmanthus, apricot, marble, temple resin
Baseolive wood, cedar, myrrh, styrax, warm musk

Musashi

Unbound 001 · Extrait de Parfum

Undefeated, then at peace.

Swordsman, philosopher, ink painter. The cold flash of a blade and thin mountain air, then ink stone and kodo incense over agarwood, settling into cave stone, hinoki and moss, warmed by a low vanilla-amber resolve.

Topblade-cold green, mountain air
Heartink stone, kodo incense, agarwood, temple resin
Basecave stone, hinoki, moss, vanilla-amber

The Spirit Collection

Spirit of The People

Extrait de Parfum · Ongoing release

As Russian as possible.

Topcoriander, black pepper, petitgrain
Heartoud, church incense, violet leaf, ginger, clove
BaseRussian leather, cured tobacco, black tea, cedar, oakmoss

Rye bread and pepper vodka on the table, a samovar of black tea, incense from the church down the street, an officer's leather satchel by the door. Opens dry and spiced, deepens into resinous smoke and cold violet leaf, settles into Russian leather, cured tobacco and black tea over mossy cedar.

Not limited, not numbered. The Spirit Collection is the house's standing line.
